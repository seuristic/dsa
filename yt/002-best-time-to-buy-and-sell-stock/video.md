Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock (LeetCode 121) explained step by step in C++, from O(n²) brute force to an O(n), O(1) space one-pass solution.

What you'll learn:
• Brute force: check every buy and later sell day. O(n²) time, O(1) space
• Prefix minimum array: cheapest price up to each day. O(n) time, O(n) space
• One pass: keep only the cheapest price so far. O(n) time, O(1) space
• The "max − min" trap: why the highest price minus the lowest price is wrong
• Edge cases: one day, falling prices, lowest price on the last day
• The pattern: best pair (i before j) → fix j, remember the best i so far
