Maximum Subarray (LeetCode 53) explained step by step in C++, from O(n³) brute force to an O(n) Kadane's algorithm solution.

What you'll learn:
• Brute force: sum every subarray from scratch. O(n³) time, O(1) space
• Running sum: fix the start, extend the end. O(n²) time, O(1) space
• Kadane's algorithm: at each number, extend or restart. O(n) time, O(1) space
• The all-negative trap: why best must start at INT_MIN, not 0
• Edge cases: one number, all negatives, zeros, dips worth keeping
• The pattern: best subarray → best one ending at each index
