Container With Most Water (LeetCode 11) explained step by step in C++, from O(n²) brute force to an O(n) two pointers solution.

What you'll learn:
• Brute force: try every pair of lines. O(n²) time, O(1) space
• Two pointers: start with the widest container, always move the shorter line inward. O(n) time, O(1) space
• Why move the shorter line: it has already given its best container, so it can be dropped for good
• Why moving the taller line never helps: width shrinks and the shorter line still caps the water
• Edge cases: two lines, zeros, equal heights, tall lines close together
• The pattern: best pair from both ends → two pointers, drop the side that can't help
