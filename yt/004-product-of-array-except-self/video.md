Product of Array Except Self (LeetCode 238) explained step by step in C++, from O(n²) brute force to an O(n) time, O(1) extra space prefix and suffix solution.

What you'll learn:
• Brute force: multiply everything except i, for every i. O(n²) time, O(1) space
• Prefix and suffix arrays: left product times right product. O(n) time, O(n) space
• Two passes: prefix stored in the answer, suffix in one variable. O(n) time, O(1) extra space
• Why division fails: zeros break "total ÷ nums[i]", and it's not allowed anyway
• Edge cases: one zero, two zeros, negatives, only two numbers
• The pattern: everything except i → left part × right part
